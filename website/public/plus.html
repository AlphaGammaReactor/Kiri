Questions about Plus

Do I need an account?

No. Your code appears on the page after payment and in your email. Paste it into Kiri.

What if I change computers?

One code works on 3 computers. A computer that hasn’t been used for 120 days frees its place by itself, or you can remove it in Settings.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days, no questions asked. Reply to the receipt email.

Is any of my data uploaded?

No. Unlocking sends only the code number and a hashed install ID, once. You can turn that off in Settings.

Can a lab get an invoice?

Yes. Enter your institution’s details at checkout and Stripe emails the invoice.

What happens if I don’t buy Plus?

Nothing changes. Kiri won’t nag you, there is no trial, and no analysis is ever locked.
