Questions

Before you download

Is Kiri really free?

Yes. Every analysis, dataset, figure export and language mode is free. Kiri Plus only adds conveniences.

Do I need an account?

No. Kiri has no accounts. Download it, open it, and start.

Can I use it for my thesis or a paper?

Yes. Each result names the method and the software package behind it, so you can cite it, and figures export at journal column widths. Check your department’s rules as well.

Why does my Mac warn me during install?

The beta isn’t notarised by Apple yet, so macOS stops the first launch. That’s expected. The install guide shows the two clicks that open it.

Does it work in mainland China?

Yes. Kiri works offline once installed. Public datasets download from their original sources, which can be slow in mainland China. A faster mirror is planned.
