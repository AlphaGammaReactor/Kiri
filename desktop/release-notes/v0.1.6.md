Update checks that keep working while Kiri stays open, plus protein data. Kiri updates itself from 0.1.5: when the banner says "Update ready. Restart to update", click it. Kiri waits if an analysis is running.

New in 0.1.6:
- Updates: Kiri used to look for a new version only when it opened and every 6 hours, so a Kiri left open could miss a release for a day. It now checks every 30 minutes, when you come back to its window, and again soon after a failed check.
- Protein on the Expression screen (Human Protein Atlas): protein in tumours compared with normal tissue (CPTAC), where in the cell the protein sits, the tissues and single cell types with the most RNA, and staining.
- Open Targets on the dependency screen: how strongly the gene is linked to the project's cancer, by type of evidence.
- About lists every bundled data source with its licence and citation.
