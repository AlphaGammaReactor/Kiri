Fixes from the first round of testing. Kiri updates itself from 0.1.1: when the banner says "Update ready. Restart to update", click it. Kiri waits if an analysis is running.

New in 0.1.2:
- New project, genes: suggestions as you type, from the official gene list built into Kiri (no internet needed). Other names such as p53 or HER2 become the official symbol (TP53, ERBB2), with a short note. Pasting a list works.
- New project says which genes are not in the bundled sample data, so you know a full cohort download is needed for them.
- A project holds several genes. In Survival and Expression they appear as one-click chips under the Gene box.
- Cancer types show as removable chips, with search, a count, Clear and Done.
- "Your research question (optional)": shown as the overview title and at the top of the exported summary. Kiri has no AI and does not read it.
- Figures fill their card and can be zoomed (buttons, pinch or ⌘-scroll), panned, and opened full window. Exported sizes are unchanged.
- The app icon's K is now centred.
- Help text under form fields is no longer bold or clipped.

Not in it yet: Word export, the figure guide, the figure board, other analyses (correlation, enrichment and so on), importing your own data, and Kiri Plus.

This build is not signed by Apple. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
