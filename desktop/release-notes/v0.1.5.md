Bench data, multi-panel figures, tutorials and more cancer biology. Kiri updates itself from 0.1.4: when the banner says "Update ready. Restart to update", click it. Kiri waits if an analysis is running.

New in 0.1.5:
- Bench data: paste qPCR results (ΔΔCt) or other measurements and get a tested figure.
- Figure board: build multi-panel figures at journal width from your saved figures.
- Tutorials: six guided walks through the real app on real data.
- Kiri, the mascot, appears on first run, in empty states, while loading and on errors (concept art for now).
- Survival: progression-free interval and disease-specific survival from the TCGA Pan-Cancer Clinical Data Resource.
- Mutation status: survival and expression split by whether the gene is mutated (GDC somatic calls).
- Copy number: expression by copy number state, with a dosage correlation (GDC ASCAT3).
- Molecular subtypes and tumour purity from the PanCanAtlas.
- Cell line dependency: do cancer cells need your gene? (DepMap 24Q4).
- Differential expression enrichment can also use GO Biological Process and WikiPathways.
- Genes the data keep under an older symbol or another name are still found.
- Fixes: differential expression after a cohort download; clearer cohort download states.
- Settings search, "Check in another cohort", and tidier buttons and cards.

Not in it yet: Kiri Plus.
