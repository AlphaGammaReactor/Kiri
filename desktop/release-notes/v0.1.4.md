New analyses and your own data. Kiri updates itself from 0.1.3: when the banner says "Update ready. Restart to update", click it. Kiri waits if an analysis is running.

New in 0.1.4:
- Import your own table: bring an expression or clinical table from Excel or CSV into a project.
- Differential expression and enrichment: compare tumour with normal or two groups of patients, with a volcano plot and pathway enrichment (MSigDB Hallmark and Reactome).
- Expression across cancers: one gene in every TCGA cancer type, with matched normal tissue from GTEx.
- Immune context: immune cell marker scores and how they relate to your gene.
- Interactions and structure: protein partners from STRING, protein facts from UniProt and the predicted structure from AlphaFold.
- Across cancers and Interactions need the internet the first time you use a gene. Only the gene symbol is sent, and the result is kept on your Mac. The sample-project genes work offline. See About > Privacy.

Not in it yet: bench data, the figure board, tutorials and Kiri Plus.

This build is not signed by Apple. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
